Your progress

Your missions, planes, currencies and settings are saved on your device, and a copy is kept on our server (cloud save). Settings → CLOUD SAVE shows when it was last saved and has SAVE NOW. On Android, tap LINK GOOGLE ACCOUNT there to keep the same progress on a new phone or after reinstalling: sign in with the same Google account and the game offers your saved progress. Without a linked account the copy belongs to this install of the game, so keep the game installed when you change settings or update your phone.

If the game finds a copy further along than the one on your phone, it asks which one to keep. The one you leave is kept on the phone for 7 days: Settings → BRING BACK.

Delete your data

In the game: Settings → DELETE ONLINE DATA removes your account from our server at once, with your callsign and scores, arena record, cloud save, mailbox and any linked Google sign-in; your progress on the device stays. You can also e-mail us from the address above with your pilot number to have it deleted for you.

Purchases

  • Items you buy are delivered at once. If something did not arrive, restart the game while online: the store hands over any purchase that is still waiting.
  • Bought Commander Lifetime Pack, Remove Ads, Gold Doubler or Growth Fund before, on this or another device? Open the game's Settings (the cog at the top left of the hangar) and tap Restore purchases.
  • Gems, packs and other consumable items are tied to the device's save and cannot be restored once spent or after the game is deleted.
  • Refunds are handled by the store you paid: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, Google Play in its Order history.
  • Items with random contents (chests, the weapon machine) show their odds in the game, under Drop rates, before you buy.

Ads and privacy choices

The game shows ads; the Remove Ads or Commander Lifetime Pack purchase turns off the ads between missions (videos you choose to watch for a reward stay available). In Europe, the UK and Switzerland you can change your consent at any time in Settings → Privacy options. On iPhone, whether apps may track you is in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. More in our Privacy Policy.

Notifications, sound and language

All are in the game's Settings. Reminders (fuel full, factory done, new daily orders) can also be turned off in your phone's settings for the game. The game never sends ads or offers as notifications.
