Sky Force: Air Strike

An arcade warplane shoot 'em up by LEAFMIND TECH LIMITED.

Fly 36 aircraft, from World War II propeller fighters to modern stealth jets, through 900 missions and 90 chapters. Drag to fly, your guns fire on their own: weave through enemy waves, collect gold, build your squadron and take down a giant boss at the end of every chapter.
