Privacy Policy — Sky Force: Air Strike

Effective 27 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Sky Force: Air Strike ("the Game", on iPhone and on Android, com.skysquadron.game) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. "We" means the developer of the Game, LEAFMIND TECH LIMITED, reachable at levantung.leafmindtech@gmail.com.

What the Game itself stores

The Game does not ask for your real name, e-mail address, phone number or a login (unless you choose to link a Google account, below). Your progress (missions, planes, currencies, settings) is stored on your device, and a copy of it is kept on our server (cloud save, below); deleting the Game deletes what is on the device.

Online features: leaderboards, the arena and real-time duels

To rank you against other pilots, the Game creates an anonymous account on our server (Google Firebase) the first time it goes online. It has no name, e-mail or password: only a random identifier. With it the server keeps:

  • the callsign you choose (or the one the Game suggests), shown to other players on the boards;
  • your country, taken from your phone's region setting (not from GPS), for the boards' country tab;
  • your game results: missions cleared, medals, the plane you fly and its power, weekly and tournament scores, arena trophies and duel results;
  • during a real-time duel, your plane's position, score and hits, passed to your opponent while the duel lasts.

Other players see your callsign, country, plane and scores on the boards and in duels, never anything else. A callsign that other players report as offensive is hidden from every board. This data is used only to run these features; it is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else.

Cloud save and Google sign-in

So that a new phone, a reinstall or a second device can take your progress back, the Game keeps a copy of your progress (the same game data it stores on the device: missions, planes, currencies, settings, with the missions cleared, squad power and gems shown beside it, the time it was saved and a random identifier of the device that saved it) on our server under your anonymous account, and updates it as you play. When another copy is further along, the Game asks you which one to keep; the other one stays on your device for 7 days so you can bring it back.

On Android you may link a Google account (Settings → LINK GOOGLE ACCOUNT) to keep the same progress on every device you sign in on. Google then shares your Google account's identifier, e-mail address and name with our Firebase Authentication, which keeps them with your account to recognise you; we use them for nothing else. Linking is optional, and the Game works without it.

Mailbox

The Game's mailbox holds messages we send to players (gifts, compensation, notices, a welcome back after a week away). For each message the server keeps whether you read it and claimed its rewards, so each reward is given only once. To choose which players a message is for, the Game tells the server your missions cleared, your country (from the phone's region setting) and your platform. Each message is kept for 90 days.

Tracking and personalised ads: your choices

  • iPhone: the Game asks for permission before ads may use your device's advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised from it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the Game shows a consent form (IAB TCF v2.2) before any ad is requested. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised and analytics storage is denied.
  • United States (states with privacy laws): you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising through the same Google form (IAB GPP).
  • You can change your consent at any time: Settings → Privacy options in the Game.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads.

Notifications

If you allow them, the Game schedules reminders on your device (fuel full, factory done, new daily orders). They are created on the device, carry no ads or offers, and can be turned off in your phone's settings.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for a general audience (parental guidance). If you believe a child has used the Game and want data removed, contact us.

In-app purchases and random items

The Game is free to play and offers optional purchases. Some items bought with in-game currency have random contents; their odds are shown in the Game (Drop rates) before every purchase.

Data retention and deletion

Data collected by Google's services is kept under Google's retention policies; Firebase Analytics user-level data is kept for at most 14 months. Deleting the Game removes everything it stored on your device.

Your online data (the anonymous account and, if you linked one, its Google sign-in, your callsign, country and every line on the boards, the arena record and duels, the cloud save and the mailbox) is kept while you play and can be deleted at any time from inside the Game: Settings → DELETE ONLINE DATA. It is removed from the server at once, and from the boards' summaries at their next refresh; your progress on the device stays. You can also e-mail us to have it deleted. To ask for data linked to your device to be deleted by the other services above, e-mail us and we will pass the request on and help you.

Security

All data sent by the Game's services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's update notes.
