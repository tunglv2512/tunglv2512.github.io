Privacy Policy — Sky Force: Air Strike

Effective 25 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Sky Force: Air Strike ("the Game", on iPhone and on Android, com.skysquadron.game) collects, how it is used and the choices you have. "We" means the developer of the Game, LEAFMIND TECH LIMITED, reachable at levantung.leafmindtech@gmail.com.

What the Game itself stores

The Game does not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or any account, and we do not run servers that receive your data. Your progress (missions, planes, currencies, settings) is stored on your device only; deleting the Game deletes it.

Tracking and personalised ads: your choices

  • iPhone: the Game asks for permission before ads may use your device's advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised from it. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch the Game shows a consent form (IAB TCF v2.2) before any ad is requested. If you refuse, you may still see ads, but they are not personalised and analytics storage is denied.
  • United States (states with privacy laws): you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your personal information and of targeted advertising through the same Google form (IAB GPP).
  • You can change your consent at any time: Settings → Privacy options in the Game.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads.

Notifications

If you allow them, the Game schedules reminders on your device (fuel full, factory done, new daily orders). They are created on the device, carry no ads or offers, and can be turned off in your phone's settings.

Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Ads in the Game are limited to content suitable for a general audience (parental guidance). If you believe a child has used the Game and want data removed, contact us.

In-app purchases and random items

The Game is free to play and offers optional purchases. Some items bought with in-game currency have random contents; their odds are shown in the Game (Drop rates) before every purchase.

Data retention and deletion

Data collected by Google's services is kept under Google's retention policies; Firebase Analytics user-level data is kept for at most 14 months. Because we hold no account or server copy, deleting the Game removes everything it stored on your device. To ask for data linked to your device to be deleted by the services above, e-mail us and we will pass the request on and help you.

Security

All data sent by the Game's services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's update notes.
